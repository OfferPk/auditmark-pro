# AuditMark Pro — User Guide & Deployment Manual

## How to Deploy Compliance in 3 Steps:

### Step 1: Configure Legal Profile

Under the **Policy Setup** tab, enter your organization's legal name, website URL, data categories collected, third-party sub-processors, and data retention windows.

### Step 2: Customize Cookie Banner

Under the **Cookie Banner** tab, choose your theme (Dark Slate, Deep Indigo, Emerald Oasis) and layout position (Bottom Bar vs Floating Box).

### Step 3: Embed & Export

1. Click **Get Banner Embed** to copy the script snippet directly into your Shopify theme, WordPress header, or Webflow custom code.  
2. Click **Download Policy (MD)** to download your compliant Privacy Policy Markdown file for publishing to your `/privacy` page.
